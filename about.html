About the Slapping Studs

We are the student ice hockey club of Enschede: four squads, one coach and about sixty people sharing the ice at IJsbaan Twente three evenings a week.

Come to a practice

Founded in 1982, still run by students

The whole history

SIJHV The Slapping Studs was founded in February 1982 and has been playing in Enschede ever since. It is the oldest student ice hockey association in the Netherlands, and it is affiliated with the University of Twente — though it has never been closed off to one school. UT and Saxion students skate here, and so do people who are not studying at all.

Fifty to sixty members are spread over four squads with one coach between them. English is the working language of the club: on the ice, at practice and in the club’s own business, because English is what the room actually has in common.

Nobody is expected to turn up knowing how to play. Most members start in the Cadets, where there is no league and nothing is assumed. The club lends skates, a helmet with a cage, gloves, pads, a stick and a neck guard, and the first practice costs nothing — you need a pair of gloves and the willingness to fall over in front of strangers.

The four squads

See the roster

Which squad you play in follows how much hockey you have behind you, not how long you have been a member. People move between them as the season goes.

Slapping Studs 1

Sixth Division North

The most experienced squad in the club, and the one that takes the hardest games. If you have played before, this is usually where you are pointed.

Slapping Studs 2

Sixth Division North

The same division as Studs 1, which means the two squads meet each other in competition — the one fixture nobody on either bench needs reminding about.

Striking Stallions

Fifth Division North

Champions of the Fifth Division North in 2026 — the first divisional title in the club’s history, won forty-four years after the first practice.

Cadets

No league

Where most people start. No league games, no experience assumed, and most of a season to learn to skate, stop and shoot before anyone asks you to do all three at once.
